--- Entry 1 | Date identified: 2025-03-31 | Location: Appendix A ---
Issue: Algorithms 9 and 10 use the zeta values for i = 1, …, 127, however Appendix A also includes the value 1 (corresponding to i = 0).
Potential correction: After the first two sentences of Appendix A, a new sentence will be added:  "The value 1, corresponding to i = 0, has been included so that i-th entry of the array would then equal 𝜁^BitRev_7(i)." 

--- Entry 2 | Date identified: 2025-10-17 | Location: Section 5.3 ---
Issue: In Algorithm 15, line 7, the comment reads "decode plaintext m from polynomial v."  However, v is not used in the algorithm.
Potential correction: The comment will be changed to read "decode plaintext m from polynomial w."
