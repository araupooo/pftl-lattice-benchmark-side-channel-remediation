ERRATA ENTRIES: 31

--- Entry 1 | Date identified: 2024-09-20 | Location: Sec. 2.5 and Sec. 7.5 ---
Issue: As written in the explanation of NTT, the polynomial w gets evaluated twice when it should be only once.  Concretely, In equations (2.1) and (7.1) the NTT is explicitly written as NTT(w)_j=w(\zeta_j) and the following line says "where \zeta_j=w(\zeta^{2BitRev_8(i)+1} ) mod q".
This would mean that each coordinate of the NTT requires evaluating the polynomial w twice, first at \zeta^{2BitRev_8(i)+1} and then again at \zeta_j.  In other words computing w ( w (\zeta^{2BitRev_8(i)+1} )).
Potential correction: The polynomial w should only be evaluated once.  One should replace “where \zeta_j=w(\zeta^{2BitRev_8(i)+1} ) mod q.” with “where \zeta_j=\zeta^{2BitRev_8(i)+1}  mod q.”  This change should occur both in section 2.5 and 7.5, immediately following equations (2.1) and (7.1).

--- Entry 2 | Date identified: 2024-10-17 | Location: Sec. 6.2 and Sec. 6.3 ---
Issue: On page 23, Section 6.2, is this sentence: “Before the message M is signed, it is concatenated with the public key hash tr and hashed down to a 64-byte message representative µ using H.” The M should be M’. The same change should also be made to the first sentence in Section 6.3: “The algorithm ML-DSA.Verify_internal (Algorithm 8) takes a public key pk encoded as a byte string, a message M encoded as a bit string, and a signature σ encoded as a byte string as input.”
Potential correction: In both sentences indicated, the variable M should be changed to M'.

--- Entry 6 | Date identified: 2025-12-02 | Location: Sec. 5 ---

--- Entry 7 | Date identified: 2025-12-02 | Location: Sec. 6 ---

--- Entry 8 | Date identified: 2025-12-02 | Location: Sec. 7 ---
Issue: Above Algorithm 28 the text says that the algorithm w1Encode is used in ML-DSA.Sign.  It is actually used in ML-DSA.Sign_internal and in ML-DSA.Verify_intenal
Potential correction: The text will be corrected to refer to the internal signing and verifying algorithms.

--- Entry 9 | Date identified: 2025-12-02 | Location: App. A ---
Issue: The version of Montgomery reduction described here differs from the one used in the Dilithium reference implementation in a few ways: (a) it incorrectly gives the upper bound of the input range as inclusive when it should be exclusive, (b) it does not clarify that t should use the "signed" representative mod 2^32 and be sign-extended, and (c) it misleadingly states that the output is less than 2q in absolute value, when in fact it's less than q in absolute value.
Potential correction: Appendix A will be rewritten to match what is in the reference implementation, fixing the points raised in the Issue Description.

--- Entry 11 | Date identified: 2026-02-27 | Location: Sec. 7.4 ---
Issue: Algorithm 40 UseHint says that it outputs a value  0 <= r1 <= (q-1) /2γ2.  This is technically correct, but the real upper bound is 1 smaller:  0 <= r1 <= (q-1) / 2γ2 - 1.  
Note that the value is passed to w1Encode during internal verification, which relies on the real upper bound and correctly documents it.
Potential correction: The bound in Algorithm 40 will be corrected to 0 <= r1 <= (q-1) / 2γ2 - 1
